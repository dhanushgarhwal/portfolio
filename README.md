## 🏠 What is this?

It's a small, dark, calm website where you can get to know me in about a minute. Who I am, what I've built, what I'm good at, and how to reach me. No clutter, no long scrolling. Just a few simple pages you flip through.

## 🧭 What's on it?

**🏡 Home**
A short intro about me, plus round buttons to find me on my other accounts or send me an email.

**💼 Projects**
Things I've built, shown as a row of cards. The one in the middle is the one in focus. Click a side card, swipe, or use the arrow buttons to bring the next one forward.

**🧠 Skills**
A sliding strip of boxes, one for each thing I know or use. The one in the middle is the one in focus, and it's drawn big. Click a side box, swipe, or use the arrow buttons to bring the next one forward.

**🛤️ Journey**
My story so far, one update at a time, from the start to today. Until the first update is added from the control room, the page simply says "nothing here yet". **A first-time visitor starts on the start marker** ("the start") and travels the story from its beginning; **someone who has opened the page before starts on my latest update**, as it glides in from the "i am here" end. The visit is remembered in a cookie, `jy-seen=1` (one year, `path=/`, `SameSite=Lax`, `Secure` on https) plus a `localStorage` copy, written the moment the Journey page opens (not on the first page load of the site) and read once when it opens; with cookies and storage both blocked every visit counts as new and nothing breaks. Inside one page load, leaving and coming back resumes where you stopped. Scroll or drag to travel along the road. It is a finite road with a real start and a real end, made only of straight stretches: some run straight down, some run sideways, and many are tilted to a random angle so the road zig-zags, joined by corners. Every road has all three kinds. **Card spacing:** every update sits on a corner, and the stretch between two updates is half the length at which one card would leave the screen just as the next arrives, so **one or two cards are on the screen at every point of the road** (never none, never three), and neighbouring cards go on opposite sides of the road where the geometry allows. This replaces the earlier "exactly one card on the screen at all times" rule; the newer rule wins. (Only where a third card would otherwise appear is a stretch lengthened, by at most half as much again; a sideways stretch is as long as the longer of its two cards' hand-off, so it holds exactly the two cards of its corners.) The spacing is worked out from the real screen size and the real height of each card. The view follows the road. The road is drawn live on a canvas from a seed, so a given seed always makes the same road. **The trail:** a crisp bright line (silver core, a very thin soft edge in the highlight colour) runs along the middle of the road from the start marker to the small bright dot that is you; it grows and shrinks as you move, in both directions, follows every corner, and nothing glows round or ahead of the dot. The road beyond the dot keeps its dim dashes. Cards sit beside each corner, on the side that keeps them off the road; the latest card is a little bigger. There is no scrollbar anywhere: use the wheel, drag with a mouse or finger, or the keys (arrows, Page Up/Down, Home, End, Space). A dark vignette, equal on all four edges, keeps attention in the middle. The page switches to its final look the moment it opens, so the plain list never flashes. The wheel, a swipe or the arrow keys (and W / S) move the road, and at the very top or the very end they change the page exactly as on every other page (a gesture that started inside the road and runs into an end only bounces, it never carries on into a page change). Only the tail of the swipe or the held key that brought you to the page is ignored; the next gesture works at once, wherever the pointer is resting.

## 🔐 My private control room

There's a hidden page, just for me, where I can change almost everything on the site without touching any of the behind-the-scenes files:

- ✏️ Edit the words on every page
- 🖼️ Swap pictures
- 🔘 Add, hide or reorder my link buttons
- 🗂️ Add or remove projects and skills, and drag them into the order I want (the top one is the one selected when the site opens)
- 🛤️ Run the Journey page: add, edit, duplicate, hide, pin or delete updates (they sort themselves by date), and tune the road
- 💌 Read the feedback people leave, pin the good ones, delete the rest
- 🚀 Press one button to make my changes go live

**The Journey section, in one line per setting:**

- **Entries:** title, date (`2024`, `2024-05` or `2024-05-17`), text, tag, icon, optional picture, optional link, which side the card sits on, visible, pinned. The newest visible one is always the "latest".
- **Page:** on/off, title, subtitle, direction (newest first or oldest first), start label, end label.
- **Road:** seed (the same seed always draws the same road; re-roll for a new one, lock to keep it), spacing between updates, swing, curviness, randomness, road width.
- **Highlight:** breathing on/off, breathing speed, colour.
- **Data:** export the whole Journey as a file, or import one (it is checked first, and nothing changes if it isn't valid).
- Each group has a reset-to-defaults button, and the **Preview** button shows the real site, Journey included, from your unsaved changes.

Only I can get in. Nobody else can see it, open it or change anything, and it's kept out of search results.
